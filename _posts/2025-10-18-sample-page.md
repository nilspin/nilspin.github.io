### Tufte's Aesthetics in Action

The Tufte theme is special because it enables **sidenotes** and **margin notes**.

To create a **sidenote**, you use the following HTML structure:

```html
<label for="sn-example" class="margin-toggle">&#x2741;</label>
<input type="checkbox" id="sn-example" class="margin-toggle"/>
<span class="marginnote">This text will appear in the margin next to the main column.</span>
